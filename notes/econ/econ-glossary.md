# Econ glossary: supply and demand

Short definitions to review before the quiz. The full lessons are in econ-supply-demand.pdf.

**Complement.** A good used together with another, like coffee and pandesal. If coffee gets more expensive, demand for its complements tends to fall.

**Demand.** How much of a good buyers are willing and able to buy at each price, over a given time.

**Equilibrium price.** The price where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. Also called the market-clearing price.

**Inferior good.** A good people buy less of when their income goes up, like instant noodles for some families.

**Law of demand.** When price goes up, quantity demanded goes down, all else equal.

**Law of supply.** When price goes up, quantity supplied goes up, all else equal.

**Normal good.** A good people buy more of when their income goes up.

**Price ceiling.** A legal maximum price. If it is below equilibrium, it causes a shortage.

**Price floor.** A legal minimum price. If it is above equilibrium, it causes a surplus.

**Shortage.** Quantity demanded is greater than quantity supplied, which happens when the price is below equilibrium.

**Substitute.** A good that can replace another, like bus rides and jeepney rides. If one gets more expensive, demand for the other goes up.

**Supply.** How much of a good sellers are willing and able to sell at each price.

**Surplus.** Quantity supplied is greater than quantity demanded, which happens when the price is above equilibrium.
